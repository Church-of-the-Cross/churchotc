Defibrillator Training Is Coming!

Did you know that Church of the Cross has an automated external defibrillator (AED)? This device is very effective in providing lifesaving treatment to persons experiencing cardiac arrest and can be used effectively by persons with minimal training, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

If you would like to be part of a group who will be trained to use AEDs, please advise a Council member. More information about the date and time of the training will be available soon.

Let's Support Stephanie Park!

Stephanie was a valued secretary for our church for many years. She supported us in our daily operations and in prayer.

Stephanie is currently working on an Anglican Studies 1-year program which requires her to live apart from her husband, cut back her work to a contract role, and do work-study while focusing on her studies.

Once completed, Stephanie will serve as a bivocational Episcopal priest in her home parish and five other small parishes that cannot afford a pastor.

She is asking us for prayer and financial aid. If you would like to donate financially, please write “Stephanie Park” in the memo line of your check made out to Church of the Cross and drop it in our donation box. We will write one check to Stephanie at the end of September.
